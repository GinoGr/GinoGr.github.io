Amenities

Thoughtful comforts for a relaxing, easy stay.

Seminary Hill Retreat is equipped to make guests feel at home from the moment they arrive, with updated interiors, practical conveniences, outdoor gathering spaces, and the small details that make a getaway feel effortless.

At a glance

Inside the home, guests can enjoy a fully equipped kitchen, high-speed Wi-Fi, smart TV streaming, central air, a pellet stove fireplace, laundry, and comfortable furnishings throughout. Outside, the property offers a spacious deck, backyard space, outdoor seating, dining areas, and a fire pit to take in the peaceful hilltop setting.

Highlights

  • Fast Wi-Fi and smart TV
  • Fully equipped kitchen
  • Washer and dryer
  • Central air conditioning and heating
  • Fireplace and outdoor fire pit
  • Spacious deck and outdoor seating
  • Garage parking and self check-in

Kitchen & dining

Full kitchen with modern appliances, cooking essentials, coffee and tea setup, dining table, dishwasher, microwave, toaster, blender, kettle, and more.

Living & entertainment

Large smart TV with streaming access, piano, books, reading material, and comfortable gathering spaces for relaxing together.

Bedrooms & laundry

Quality linens, extra pillows and blankets, room-darkening shades, hangers, clothing storage, and an in-home washer and dryer.

Heating & cooling

Central air conditioning, ceiling fans, heating, and a pellet stove fireplace help keep the home comfortable in every season.

Outdoor spaces

Private entrance, deck, backyard, outdoor dining area, lounge seating, and a fire pit for taking in the surrounding views and fresh air.

Parking & convenience

Two garage spaces, additional onsite parking, smart-lock self check-in, and a single-level layout with no interior stairs.

Family-friendly touches

Children’s dinnerware, games, books, and practical spaces that make the home comfortable for family stays.

Safety features

Smoke and carbon monoxide alarms, exterior lighting, fire extinguisher, first aid kit, and outdoor monitoring features for added peace of mind.

Guest extras

Complimentary coffee and tea, essentials like towels and toiletries, and optional cleaning during longer stays.

Designed for comfort

The amenities here are meant to support both restful weekends and longer stays. Whether guests are cooking a meal, streaming a movie, enjoying a quiet morning coffee outside, or settling in after a day exploring Centralia and the surrounding area, the home is set up to feel simple, warm, and easy to enjoy.

Good to know

  • Non-smoking property
  • No pets
  • No parties without prior approval
  • Self check-in with smart lock
  • Outdoor security features are in place
  • Ample onsite parking is available
