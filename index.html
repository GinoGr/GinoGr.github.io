About the stay

Seminary Hill Retreat is a private vacation rental in Centralia, Washington

Location & Area

Enjoy a scenic drive along a quiet country road lined with old-growth forest, towering Douglas fir, and cedar trees as you arrive at this peaceful hilltop retreat. Just minutes away, the charming town of Centralia offers a unique blend of history, small-town character, and local attractions.

Founded in 1875 by George Washington, one of the first Black pioneers in Washington State, Centralia has a rich and distinctive history that still shapes the town today. Visitors can explore its historic downtown, filled with antique shops, local dining, and classic architecture.

Nearby, the Seminary Hill Natural Area offers miles of forested trails, scenic viewpoints, and a quiet escape into nature—perfect for hiking, birdwatching, and taking in the beauty of the Pacific Northwest.

Learn more about the town at Centralia’s official website .
