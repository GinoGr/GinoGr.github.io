About the stay

The best vacation rental in Centralia, Washington

A private, custom-built cedar home set on 5 peaceful acres, Seminary Hill Retreat offers a quiet hilltop escape surrounded by forest, open meadow, and beautiful mountain views. Guests can enjoy modern, brand-new interiors, first-class amenities, and the freedom to explore the orchard and scenic grounds while relaxing far from the noise of city life.
